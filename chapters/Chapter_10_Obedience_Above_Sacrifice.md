## 10.1 The question

The chapter's question is the *kind of being* the *prophetic* critique is. The chapter does *not* argue the *prophets* reject *sacrifice* as an institution; the chapter argues the *prophets* reject *sacrifice* that is *disconnected* from the *obedience* the *covenant* is for. The chapter's *acceptance criterion* is the *what kind of obedience does God require?* question — the *prophetic* critique is the *kind of being* the *required* obedience is.

The chapter confines itself to the *prophetic* critique. The *Levitical* legislation is the *sacred* institution that the *prophets* are *not* rejecting — the *prophets* are rejecting the *sacrifice* that is *disconnected* from the *Levitical* *framework*. The chapter treats the *prophetic* critique as the *kind of being* the *sacrifice* is — the *kind of being* that *sacrifices* without *repenting*.

**Translation notes:**

1. **1 Samuel 15:22** "to obey is better than sacrifice" (Hebrew *ṭôb šəmā‘ miḥ̂at-hāmeḥ* / Hebrew *hāneq* / Hebrew *ṭôb*) — the *to obey* is the *kind of being* the *obedience* is; the *sacrifice* is the *kind of being* the *sacrifice* is. The *better* (Hebrew *ṭôb*) is the *preference* — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *obedience* is, *not* the *kind of being* the *sacrifice* is. The chapter reads the *better* as the *preference* — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *obedience* is.

2. **Hosea 6:6** "I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice" (Hebrew *ḥesed* / Hebrew *zebaḥ*) — the *steadfast love* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for*; the *sacrifice* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *not* the *primary* priority of. The chapter reads the *steadfast love* as the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for*; the *sacrifice* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *not* the *primary* priority of.

3. **Matthew 9:13** "I desire mercy and not sacrifice" (Greek *eleos* / Greek *thysia*) — the *mercy* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for*; the *sacrifice* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *not* the *primary* priority of. The chapter reads the *Jesus* reading as the *post-canonical* *reaffirmation* of the *prophetic* critique — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *mercy* is.

4. **Amos 5:21–24** "let justice roll down like waters" — the *justice* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for*; the *kind of being* the *justice* is the *kind of being* the *water* is (the *flow*), the *kind of being* the *ever-flowing* *stream* is (the *constant*). The chapter reads the *justice* as the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *justice* is, in the *form* of the *water* and the *ever-flowing* *stream*.

5. **Micah 6:8** "to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God" — the *do justice* is the *kind of being* the *justice* is; the *love kindness* is the *kind of being* the *kindness* is; the *walk humbly* is the *kind of being* the *humility* is. The chapter reads the *Micah 6:8* passage as the *direct* answer to the *what kind of obedience does God require?* question — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *justice*, *kindness*, and *humility* is. The three are *kindred* — the *kind of being* the *covenant* is *for* is the *kind of being* the *kindness* is, the *kind of being* the *humility* is, the *kind of being* the *justice* is.
