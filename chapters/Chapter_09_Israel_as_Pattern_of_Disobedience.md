## 9.1 The question

The chapter's question is whether *Israel's history* is the *narrative* evidence of the *pattern* of *disobedience* that Chapter 8 establishes. The chapter's argument is the *pattern* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* *recipient* is — the *kind of being* the *Israel* nation is the *kind of being* the *bent* direction is, *even after* the *covenant* has been established. The chapter does *not* claim the *covenant* is *ineffective*; the chapter claims the *covenant* is the *kind of being* the *recipient* is *constitutionally* in the *bent* direction that the *covenant* is *for*. The chapter treats the *pattern* as the *kind of being* the *covenant* *recipient* is.

The chapter's *acceptance criterion* is explicit: the chapter sets up Part IV by showing *why a faithful human redeemer is needed* at the *narrative* level. The chapter does *not* attempt to *resolve* the *individual* accountability question (the *Ezekiel 18* question) — the chapter treats the *individual* accountability as the *complement* of the *constitutional* transmission that the *Israel* nation is the *recipient* of.

## 9.2 The golden calf: the foundational failure (Exodus 32)

> "When the people saw that Moses delayed to come down from the mountain, the people gathered themselves together to Aaron and said to him, 'Up, make us gods who shall go before us. As for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.'" (Exodus 32:1, ESV; verified against esv.org) `[E]`

> "And he received the gold from their hand and fashioned it with a graving tool and made a golden calf. And they said, 'These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt!'" (Exodus 32:4, ESV; verified against esv.org) `[E]`

> "When Aaron saw this, he built an altar before it. And Aaron made a proclamation and said, 'Tomorrow shall be a feast to the Lord.' And they rose up early the next day and offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings. And the people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play." (Exodus 32:5–6, ESV; verified against esv.org) `[E]`

The *delay* language is the *kind of being* the *Israel* nation is — the *kind of being* that *does not wait* for the *mediator*. The *make us gods* language is the *kind of being* the *Israel* nation is — the *kind of being* that *demands* a *visible* mediator. The *golden calf* is the *kind of being* the *mediator* is — the *kind of being* that is *visible* and *controllable*. The *feast to the Lord* is the *kind of being* the *worship* is — the *kind of being* that *conflates* the *golden calf* with the *Lord*. The *play* language is the *kind of being* the *worship* *becomes* — the *kind of being* the *play* is.

The chapter treats the *passage* as the *foundational* explicit textual witness to the *Israel* failure — the *kind of being* the *Israel* nation is the *kind of being* the *golden calf* is. The chapter rates the *kind of being* the *Israel* nation is as `[E]` for the textual evidence; the *interpretation* as the *pattern* of the *covenant* *recipient* is `[I-high]` (the *textual* evidence is the *narrative*; the *interpretation* as the *pattern* is the chapter's reading).

## 9.10 The Ezekiel 18:20 limit

**Translation notes:**

1. **Deuteronomy 9:6** "stubborn people" (Hebrew *‘am-qəšeh-‘ōrep̄*) — the *stubborn* is the *kind of being* the *neck* is — the *kind of being* that is *resistant* to the *yoke*. The chapter reads the *stubborn* as the *kind of being* the *neck* is — the *kind of being* that is *resistant* to the *divine* *direction*.

2. **Psalm 78:8** "stubborn and rebellious generation" (Hebrew *sōrēr ū-mōreh*) — the *stubborn* and *rebellious* are the *kind of being* the *generation* is — the *kind of being* that is *resistant* and *rebellious*. The chapter reads the *stubborn* and *rebellious* as the *kind of being* the *generation* is — the *kind of being* that is *resistant* to the *divine* *direction*.

3. **Acts 7:42** "God turned away and gave them over to worship the host of heaven" — the *divine* *handing over* is the *kind of being* the *covenant* *disobedience* is. The chapter reads the *handing over* as the *divine* *consequence* of the *covenant* *disobedience* — the *kind of being* the *covenant* *recipient* is *handed over* to the *bent* direction.

4. **Ezekiel 18:20** — the *individual* accountability is the *complement* of the *constitutional* transmission. The chapter treats the *individual* accountability as the *limit* on the *constitutional* transmission — the *son* is *not* forensically accountable for the *father's* sin. The *constitutional* transmission is the *kind of being* the *descendants* are; the *individual* accountability is the *forensic* judgment that each individual *also* faces.
