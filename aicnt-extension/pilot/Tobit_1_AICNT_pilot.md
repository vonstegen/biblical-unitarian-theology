**Tobit**

## Chapter 1

1 Book of the words of Tobit son of Tobiel son of Ananiel son of Adouel son of Gabael [son of Raphael son of Ragouel],<sup>a</sup> of the seed of Asiel, of the tribe of Naphtali,

2 who was taken captive in the days of Enemessar the king of the Assyrians from Thisbe, which is on the right<sup>b</sup> of Kydios of Naphtali in {Upper Galilee}<sup>c</sup> above Aser, [behind the road<sup>d</sup> of the setting of the sun, on the left of Phogor].<sup>e</sup>

3 I, Tobit, walked in ways of truth and in righteousnesses all the days of my life, and I did many acts of mercy to my brothers and to my nation, to those who went with me in the captivity into the land of the Assyrians, to Nineveh.

4 And when I was in my land, in the land of Israel, and when I was young, all the tribe of Naphtali of my father {departed from the house of David my father and from Jerusalem},<sup>f</sup> the city that was chosen out of all the tribes of Israel for all the tribes of Israel to sacrifice, and the temple of the dwelling of {God}<sup>g</sup> was consecrated and was built [in it]<sup>h</sup> for all the generations of the age.

5 All my brothers and the house of Nefthalim of my father, they sacrificed to {the calf which Ierobeam the king of Israel made in Dan},<sup>i</sup> [on all the mountains of Galilee].<sup>j</sup>

6 And I, most alone, used to go often to Jerusalem at the feasts, as it is written in all Israel in an everlasting ordinance, having the firstfruits and {the firstborn and the tithes of the cattle}<sup>k</sup> and the first shearings of the sheep, I used to run off to Jerusalem.

7 And I used to give them to the priests, the sons of Aarōn, at the altar; and {the tithe of the grain and of the wine and of oil and of pomegranates and of the figs and of the remaining tree-fruits},<sup>l</sup> to the sons of Leui who minister in Jerusalem; and the second tithe I used to tithe {in money for the six years},<sup>m</sup> and I used to go and spend them in Jerusalem each year.

8 And I used to give them to {the orphans and the widows and the proselytes who were joined to the sons of Israel};<sup>n</sup> I brought them in and gave to them in the third year, and [we ate them according to the ordinance commanded concerning them in the law of Mōsēs and according to the commandments which]<sup>o</sup> Debbōra, the mother of Ananiēl our father, commanded, because my father left me an orphan and died.

9 And when I became a man, I took {a wife}<sup>p</sup> from the seed of our family and begot from her {a son and called his name}<sup>q</sup> Tobias.

10 After I was taken captive to the Assyrians, and when I was taken captive to Nineveh, I walked, and all my brothers and those from my race were eating from the breads of the nations.

11 But I guarded my soul from eating from the breads of the nations.

12 And when I remembered my God with my whole soul,

13 And the Most High gave me grace and form before Enemessaros, and {I bought for him all things for the use}<sup>r</sup>

14 And I went into Media and bought for him from there until he died, and I deposited with {Gabaelos, the brother of Gabri},<sup>s</sup> {bags in the land of Media},<sup>t</sup> ten talents of silver

15 And when Enemassar died and Sennacherim his son reigned in his place, and {the roads of Media revolted},<sup>u</sup> and I was no longer able to go into Media

16 In the days of Enemessaros I did many alms to my brothers, those of my race

17 My bread I was giving to those who hunger and garments to the naked, and if anyone of those from my nation I saw dead and thrown behind the wall of Nineveh, I buried him.

18 And if anyone Sennacherib killed when he came back fleeing from Judea [in the days of the judgment which the king of heaven executed against him concerning the blasphemies which he blasphemed],<sup>v</sup> I buried; for he killed many [of the sons of Israel]<sup>w</sup> in his wrath, and {I stole their bodies and buried them, and Sennacherib sought them and did not find them}.<sup>x</sup>

19 And a certain one of those from Nineveh went and informed the king concerning me, that I bury them, and I was hidden; and when I knew [that the king knew concerning me]<sup>y</sup> and that I was being sought to be put to death, {I feared and ran away}.<sup>z</sup>

20 And all that belonged to me was seized, and nothing was left to me [that was not taken up into the royal treasury]<sup>aa</sup> except Anna my wife and Tobia my son.

21 And {forty}<sup>ab</sup> days did not pass until his two sons killed him and fled to the mountains of Ararat; and Sacherdonos his son reigned after him. And he appointed Achicharos the son of Anael, the son of my brother, over all the accounting of his kingdom, and {he had authority over all the administration}.<sup>ac</sup>

22 Then Achicharos made request concerning me, and I went down to Nineveh. For Achicharos was the chief cupbearer and over the ring and administrator and accountant [under Sennacherim king of the Assyrians];<sup>ad</sup> and Sacherdonos appointed him second. Now he was {my cousin and of my kin}.<sup>ae</sup>

FOOTNOTES
31

a 1:1 son of Raphael son of Ragouel — Absent from GI (B A V 990 and most cursives). The Qumran Aramaic and Hebrew fragments of Tobit (4QTob<sup>a–d</sup>, 4QTob<sup>e</sup>) agree generally with the longer form (GII) where they overlap.

b 1:2 right … left — The directions are compass points; in the underlying Semitic text "right" is south and "left" is north (NETS notes).

c 1:2 Upper — Some manuscripts read "Galilee." GI (B A V 990).

d 1:2 the road — Absent from GI. The word follows the Göttingen edition (Hanhart). NETS omits it, reporting that Codex Sinaiticus does not contain it.

e 1:2 behind the road … of Phogor — Absent from GI (B A V 990).

f 1:4 departed from the house of David my father and from Jerusalem — Some manuscripts read "revolted from the house of Jerusalem." GI (B A V 990).

g 1:4 God — Some manuscripts read "the Most High." GI.

h 1:4 in it — Absent from GI.

i 1:5 the calf which Ierobeam the king of Israel made in Dan — Some manuscripts read "Baal the heifer." GI. GI also adds "all the tribes that had joined in apostasy and" at the beginning of the verse.

j 1:5 on all the mountains of Galilee — Absent from GI.

k 1:6 the firstborn and the tithes of the cattle — Some manuscripts read "the tithes of the produce." GI.

l 1:7 the tithe of the grain … tree-fruits — Some manuscripts read "the tithe of all the produce." GI.

m 1:7 in money for the six years — Some manuscripts read "I would sell." GI.

n 1:8 the orphans and the widows and the proselytes who were joined to the sons of Israel — Some manuscripts read "those to whom it was due." GI.

o 1:8 we ate them … which — Absent from GI. GI reads "as Deborah the mother of my father commanded, because I was left an orphan by my father."

p 1:9 a wife — GI adds the name: "Anna a wife."

q 1:9 a son and called his name — GI reads "and I begot from her Tobias."

r 1:13 I bought for him all things for the use — Some manuscripts read "I was his buyer." GI.

s 1:14 Gabaelos, the brother of Gabri — Some manuscripts read "Gabael the brother of Gabria." GI.

t 1:14 bags in the land of Media — Some manuscripts read "in Rages of Media." GI.

u 1:15 the roads of Media revolted — Some manuscripts read "his roads became unstable." GI.

v 1:18 in the days of the judgment … which he blasphemed — Absent from GI.

w 1:18 of the sons of Israel — Absent from GI.

x 1:18 and I stole their bodies and buried them, and Sennacherib sought them and did not find them — Some manuscripts read "I buried them by stealth … and the bodies were sought by the king and were not found." GI.

y 1:19 that the king knew concerning me — Absent from GI.

z 1:19 I feared and ran away — Some manuscripts read "I was afraid and withdrew." GI.

aa 1:20 that was not taken up into the royal treasury — Absent from GI.

ab 1:21 forty — Some manuscripts read "fifty." GI (B A V 990).

ac 1:21 and he had authority over all the administration — Some manuscripts read "and over all the administration." GI.

ad 1:22 under Sennacherim king of the Assyrians — Absent from GI.

ae 1:22 my cousin and of my kin — Some manuscripts read "my nephew." GI.
