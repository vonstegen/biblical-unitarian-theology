# Research & Writing Checklist

> A task-level breakdown of `01_Architecture_and_Outline.md`. Each chapter is
> decomposed into research, exegesis, writing, and acceptance-criteria tasks.
> Use `[E]` explicit, `[I]` strong implication, `[H]` hypothesis tags on every
> claim you draft, per the methodology in `04_Review_and_Recommendations.md`.

## How to use this file

- **One chapter per row group.** Start at Part I, do not skip ahead.
- **Tick a task only when the artifact exists** — a paragraph for exegesis,
  a markdown draft for writing, an answered question for research.
- **Foundational chapters block downstream ones.** If Part I or II is unfinished,
  every Part III–V chapter is provisional.
- **Discrepancy note.** `04_Review_and_Recommendations.md` proposed an 18-chapter
  Version 1.0 outline with appendices. This checklist follows the 16-chapter
  outline in `01_Architecture_and_Outline.md` (the current document of record).
  Reconcile the two before writing Chapters 14–16 in earnest.
