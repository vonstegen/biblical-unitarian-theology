# Debt-Debtor Thesis of Redemption - Master Project Overview

**Working Thesis:**

Humanity incurred a debt to God through Adam's disobedience. God established death as the penalty for sin. This project investigates whether Scripture presents redemption as requiring a perfectly obedient human representative whose willing obedience culminated in sacrificial death, and whether Christ's obedient life and death are inseparable aspects of redemption.

## Core Research Questions

- Who is God according to Scripture, and how does His character define justice?
- What exactly did Adam lose?
- What passed from Adam to humanity?
- Why must the Redeemer be human?
- Does Scripture present Christ's active obedience and sacrificial death as inseparable?
- Why is one righteous man's obedience sufficient for many?
- What is the relationship between the Father's will and Jesus' will?
- What role do innocence, children, and moral accountability play?
- How does Israel's history of disobedience prepare for the Messiah?
- Does the Old Testament teach that obedience is greater than sacrifice?
