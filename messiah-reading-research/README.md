# Jesus the Promised Messiah: Reading & Research Program

**Proposal v0.1 · 2026-11-10 · Awaiting owner review**

A 365-day, 66-book Revised English Version (REV) Bible reading plan and a Scripture-first study of Jesus as the promised prophet like Moses, Davidic Messiah, and appointed King. Read Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John individually, in sequence; no daily Gospel harmonization.

## Unified program architecture (proposed)

This track belongs to the proposed [Biblical Theology Research Program](../BIBLICAL_THEOLOGY_PROGRAM.md), governed by [ADR-0001](../docs/adr/0001-unified-biblical-theology-repository.md) **after owner approval**. The repository is the shared home, but the Debt–Debtor thesis, AICNT extension, and Messiah research remain independently versioned and separately accepted. No existing manuscript or AICNT extension files are moved or modified by this proposal.

## Repository boundaries
This is a **new research track**, not a revision of the root Debt–Debtor thesis. Preserve its accepted chapters and workflow. The existing `aicnt-extension/` is a **separate project** with an OT critical-edition format specification, OT plan, and Tobit pilot; reuse its findings by reference rather than duplicating or claiming to have independently validated them.

## Review policy
Feature branch → PR linked to issue → human review → owner approval → merge. A merged draft is not necessarily an accepted scholarly conclusion. Changes to textual claims require provenance, competing interpretations, and a verification record. Do not redistribute copyrighted editions without rights review.
