# REV 365-Day Reading Plan — v0.1

The plan follows **two independent continuous tracks**: Old Testament Genesis–Malachi and New Testament Matthew–Revelation. Each book is read in chapter order. The four Gospels are read **Matthew, then Mark, then Luke, then John**, without alternating accounts of the same events.

## Exact pacing
- OT: 929 chapters over 365 days. 199 days have 3 chapters; 166 days have 2 chapters.
- NT: 260 chapters. One NT chapter on days 1–5 of each 7-day cycle, for 52 cycles; day 365 is OT-only.
- The canonical enumeration is `reading-plan-365.csv`. Day numbers are independent of calendar dates.
- A day's OT reading can finish one book and begin the next. NT remains a single chapter per reading day.
- These simultaneous OT and NT assignments are **not automatically thematic pairs**.

## Daily study practice
1. Read the assigned REV chapters and note each author's main point before consulting commentary.
2. When warranted, investigate a Hebrew Scriptures passage quoted or alluded to by Jesus or an apostle; optional research does not replace canonical reading.
3. Compare REV commentary, Finnegan's reading guidance, and AICNT *after* the initial reading.
4. Record what the text states, what is inferred, and what remains uncertain.

## Research themes
Prophet like Moses (Deut 18 / Acts 3); Davidic Messiah (2 Sam 7 / Luke 1); Adam and faithful humanity (Gen 1–3 / Rom 5 / 1 Cor 15); new covenant (Jer 31 / Luke 22); resurrection and renewed creation (Dan 12 / Rev 21–22).

## Acceptance tests
365 unique days; exactly 929 OT and 260 NT chapter assignments; no missing/duplicated chapters; NT only on five days per full week; Gospel books contiguous and ordered. Cross-references must be documented as intentional rather than generated from calendar proximity.
