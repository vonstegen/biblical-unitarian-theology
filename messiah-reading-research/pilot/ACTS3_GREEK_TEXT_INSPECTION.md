## Clause-level provisional analysis

| Expression | Initial parsing / grammatical observation | Verification needed |
|---|---|---|
| `Προφήτην ὑμῖν ἀναστήσει` | `Προφήτην` accusative singular, object of `ἀναστήσει` (future active indicative, third singular); `ὑμῖν` dative plural | Confirm semantic range of `ἀνίστημι` in Greek Deut and Acts |
| `κύριος ὁ θεὸς` | Nominative subject phrase in the cited speech; source identifies God as the one who raises the prophet | Compare exact Greek Deut and Hebrew divine-name rendering |
| `ἐκ τῶν ἀδελφῶν ὑμῶν ὡς ἐμέ` | Origin from among the addressees' brothers, comparison with Moses | Establish Hebrew and LXX correspondence; avoid inferring an ontology claim from this clause alone |
| `αὐτοῦ ἀκούσεσθε` | `ἀκούσεσθε` future middle indicative, second plural, of `ἀκούω`; `αὐτοῦ` genitive singular | Determine force in context: future prediction, obligation, or command-like quotation |
| `κατὰ πάντα ὅσα ἂν λαλήσῃ` | Universal scope formula; `λαλήσῃ` aorist active subjunctive third singular with `ἄν` | Check syntax and compare source text |
| `ἥτις ἂν μὴ ἀκούσῃ` | Indefinite relative construction with subjunctive: whichever person does not listen | Compare Greek scriptural parallels |
| `ἐξολεθρευθήσεται ἐκ τοῦ λαοῦ` | Future passive indicative third singular, describing removal/destruction from the people | Check exact LXX wording of Deut 18:19, Lev 23:29, Num 15:30–31 before attributing a source |

**Status:** These are provisional grammatical observations, not peer-reviewed lexical conclusions. No Hebrew Deuteronomy, LXX Deuteronomy, Samaritan Pentateuch, or DSS reading is asserted here.

## What can and cannot yet be concluded

- **[T] Source-text observation:** The edited Acts text presents God as raising a prophet and instructs Israel to heed him, with a consequence for refusal in verse 23.
- **[I] Provisional interpretation:** Peter's application to Jesus situates him within the promised prophetic role; the precise relationship to the original Deuteronomy context remains to be studied.
- **[Q] Unresolved:** Whether Acts 3:23 combines Deut 18:19 with a formula from Lev 23:29 or Num 15:30–31, or reflects another compositional practice. No verdict without comparison of actual Greek texts.
- **[Q] Unresolved:** Which manuscript readings underlie the SBLGNT editorial differences; the SBL edition is not itself an exhaustive ancient-manuscript apparatus.
- **[Q] Unresolved:** The extent to which this passage alone supports particular Unitarian or Trinitarian Christological conclusions. No doctrinal adjudication is made here.
