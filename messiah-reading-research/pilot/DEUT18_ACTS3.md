# Pilot 001 — The Prophet Like Moses

**v0.1 · Research outline · NOT a completed manuscript collation**

## Primary passages
Deuteronomy 18:9–22 (especially 18:15–19) and Acts 3:12–26 (especially 3:22–23).

## Initial textual observations
- **[T]** Deuteronomy promises that God will raise up a prophet from among Israel and place His words in that prophet's mouth.
- **[T]** Peter applies the promise to Jesus in Acts 3.
- **[Q]** Does Deuteronomy describe a continuing prophetic office, a climactic individual, or both?
- **[Q]** Is Acts 3:23 a combination of Deuteronomy 18:19 with Leviticus 23:29, Numbers 15:30–31, or another scriptural tradition? Verify the Hebrew and Greek wording before deciding.
- **[Q]** What does the identification establish about Jesus' authority and relationship to the God who sends him? What does it leave unresolved?

## Work packages
1. Parse Hebrew Deut 18 and compare the Greek Septuagint.
2. Analyze original context and historical expectations of a prophet like Moses.
3. Establish the wording of Acts 3:22–23 from cited Greek editions.
4. Align quotations/allusions phrase by phrase and test proposed source combinations.
5. Compare Jewish, Unitarian, and Trinitarian interpretations and their strongest objections.
6. Publish a documented finding with source-level citations and unresolved questions.

**Important:** Differences in how Acts quotes Deuteronomy are not necessarily manuscript variants. Do not assert any witness reading without verifying the named witness.
