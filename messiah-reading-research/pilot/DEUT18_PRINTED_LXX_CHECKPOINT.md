## Apparatus checkpoint [T/Q]

The printed apparatus on p. 614 explicitly discusses the 18:18 phrase beginning with “my words,” including a singular alternative, and discusses omission of `ἐκεῖνος` in 18:19. Thus the edition records textual variation at these loci; differences cannot simply be dismissed as missing accents.

However, the manuscript sigla, their relationships, and all supporting witnesses have **not** been fully decoded and independently checked. This note assigns no individual manuscript reading and does not establish which edition or witness supplied BTB's text. No record is promoted to `verified`.

## Relevance to Acts [I]

Acts 3:23 has “that prophet” in shared record **EV-ACTS3-SBLGNT-001 revision 1**. Its presence in the inspected Greek Deuteronomy running text shows that this expression need not be treated as an addition unique to Acts.

The larger difference remains: Greek Deuteronomy here retains God's judgment formula, whereas Acts 3:23 uses destruction/removal from the people. The newly checked phrases do not settle the Leviticus-versus-Numbers explanation.
